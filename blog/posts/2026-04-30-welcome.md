This is where I'll be writing longer pieces on the things I work on day-to-day:
enterprise architecture, AI-native systems, and the messy middle of large-scale
transformation.

Most of what I share goes out on LinkedIn first. Those posts get cross-linked
here so everything lives in one place — the writing I'm proud of, plus the
shorter takes that earn enough engagement to be worth keeping.

A few of the things I expect to write about:

- **Architecture under uncertainty.** Most enterprise architecture is taught
  for steady-state, and almost none of it is. I'll write about how we make
  decisions when the inputs are still moving.
- **AI in regulated industries.** I lead AI work at Sonnedix, a global asset
  manager in renewables. The interesting questions aren't "can we use AI" —
  they're about audit trails, model governance, and building agentic systems
  that a regulator would sign off on.
- **The transformation paradox.** Why the same five things keep coming up in
  every transformation programme, regardless of industry, and why "people,
  process, technology" is dangerously close to a non-answer.

If you've found something I wrote useful, the easiest way to keep up is to
[follow me on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tarunbulchandani/).
This blog gets updated whenever LinkedIn does — and occasionally with
longer-form pieces that don't fit there.

— Tarun
