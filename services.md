## What I do

I build websites that small businesses can actually be found through. Not just on Google, but on ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and the growing list of AI search interfaces that an increasing share of your prospects now use.

Most small business websites in 2026 are invisible to AI search. Either they're built on template platforms whose architecture doesn't support the necessary structured data, or they're built by freelancers who haven't yet adjusted their craft to the AI-search era. The gap between what's possible and what's standard practice is substantial.

My work closes that gap, in the same way I close architectural gaps in my day-to-day enterprise work. The discipline is the same. The deliverable is a website that performs the AI-search role correctly from day one, with the structured data, the semantic clarity, and the content depth that AI assistants reward.

## Packages

Fixed scope, fixed price, no hourly surprises. Four tiers covering the full lifecycle from "is my existing site any good" to "build me a new one from scratch".

### AI Discoverability Assessment: £499

A written audit of your existing website for AI search discoverability. Covers schema.org markup, llms.txt, FAQ structure, content density, semantic HTML, and the broader signals AI assistants check.

Deliverables:

- Written audit report (typically 8 to 12 pages)
- Prioritised fix list with effort estimates
- 30-minute call to walk through findings
- Recommended next step (often the Retrofit tier)

Turnaround: one week.

Best for: small business owners who want to know where they stand before committing to a larger investment.

### AI Discoverability Retrofit: £2,495

Implementation of the audit findings against your existing site. Adds the technical baseline for AI discoverability without requiring a rebuild.

Deliverables:

- Full schema.org markup (Organization, LocalBusiness, Service, Person, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList)
- `llms.txt` at your domain root
- FAQ sections on key pages with proper markup
- Canonical URL implementation
- Sitemap and robots.txt review
- One round of content refinement for factual density
- Validation against Google Rich Results Test
- Written summary of changes

Turnaround: two weeks.

Best for: small business owners with an existing site they're otherwise happy with, who want the AI-discoverability layer added.

### AI-Ready Website Build: £4,995

Ground-up custom build. Five to eight pages, designed and built from scratch, with the full AI-discoverability stack baked in from day one.

Deliverables:

- 5 to 8 custom pages (home, about, services, contact, plus 1 to 4 service-specific or content pages)
- Custom design, not template-based
- Full schema.org markup throughout
- `llms.txt` at the root
- FAQ markup on relevant pages
- Cloudflare hosting setup (your own domain, your own account)
- Basic on-page SEO
- One round of content review and refinement
- 30-day post-launch support window

Turnaround: four to six weeks.

Best for: small business owners who are ready for a new website and want it done properly the first time.

### AI-Ready Authority Build: £9,995

Everything in the Build tier, plus the content engine that turns the site into an active marketing asset.

Deliverables:

- Everything in the Build package
- Blog setup with proper categorisation, RSS feed, topic-hub architecture
- Initial content strategy and topic map for your specialism
- Five substantive seed blog posts (1,200 to 1,800 words each, written specifically for your business)
- Topic-cluster cross-linking discipline established
- Three-month post-launch support window
- One quarterly review at month three

Turnaround: six to eight weeks.

Best for: small business owners committed to using their website as a primary marketing channel and looking for an authority platform from day one.

### Authority Maintenance Retainer: £1,200/month

Ongoing optimisation. Designed to be cancellable monthly, with no long-term commitment.

Deliverables (per month):

- AI search result monitoring against tracked queries
- Schema and structured-data tuning as the standards evolve
- Two blog posts (1,200 to 1,800 words each), written for your business
- Quarterly content audit
- Annual technical audit
- Slack or email channel for ongoing technical queries

Best for: small business owners who have a site they're happy with and want it continuously maintained at the AI-discoverability frontier.

## How engagements work

Discovery starts with a 30-minute call to understand your business, your current site, and what you're trying to achieve. After the call, you receive a written engagement scope with the deliverables, the timeline, and the fixed price.

If we proceed, a 50% deposit secures the engagement start date. The balance is due on delivery.

I take on two to three active Build or Authority engagements at any time, plus a handful of Audits and Retrofits running in parallel. I don't outsource. The work is delivered by me, directly, end to end.

## What I don't do

For clarity:

- I don't build e-commerce stores or membership sites. There are better tools and better specialists for those.
- I don't do paid acquisition or PPC management.
- I don't take on engagements where the client primarily wants a particular visual style replicated. The visual quality of the deliverable will be high, but the engineering and discoverability is what I'm selling.
- I don't compete on price. If you're looking for a £500 website, I'm not the right person.
