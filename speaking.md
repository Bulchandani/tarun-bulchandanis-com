## Topics I'm happy to speak about

**AI-native enterprise architecture in regulated industries.** What
it actually takes to ship agentic AI systems inside a regulated
enterprise — the audit instrumentation, the data-residency story,
the governance discipline, the policy work, the build-versus-buy
calculus on EA tooling.

**Building an enterprise architecture function from zero.** The
operating model decisions, the governance forum design, the
ADR practice, the standards approach. Drawn from doing this twice
across different organisational contexts.

**Large-scale ERP transformation, from the architect's seat.**
Specifically S/4HANA, but the lessons travel. What the foundational
decisions are, where the integration architecture matters most,
why the data is harder than the process, how the governance model
needs to be designed for the years after the programme.

**Acquisition-heavy companies and the architecture function inside
them.** The PE-roll-up pattern. Why textbook EA practice doesn't
fit. The 30-60-90-180 framework. Vendor consolidation as architecture
practice.

**Architecture governance in 2026.** The role of the Architecture
Governance Board now that AI-assisted authorship has changed the
cost structure of architectural documentation. The relationship
between architecture and security as identity-first security
becomes the dominant model.

## Format

I'm comfortable across formats:

- **Keynote** (30–45 minutes plus Q&A)
- **Panel** (with two to five panellists, moderated)
- **Workshop** (half-day or full-day, working through a specific
  framework with the audience)
- **Fireside** (in-conversation with a host)
- **Roundtable** (small-group executive discussion, ten to twenty
  participants)

## Inviting me

The shortest path is a message via
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tarunbulchandani/) or the
[contact form](/#contact). Include:

- The event, date and location
- The audience (who is in the room, how many, what they do)
- The format and duration you have in mind
- The topic, or the question you want the talk to answer

I'm London-based and travel for the right events. I prioritise
events with architects, CIOs, CTOs and Chief Architects in the
audience over events for vendors and the broader analyst
community.
