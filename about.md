## What I do, in a sentence

I lead enterprise architecture functions. The current iteration of
that is at Sonnedix, a global renewable-energy independent power
producer, where I established the architecture function from zero
and have personally designed and built two AI-native internal
platforms — [Meridian](/blog/case-study-meridian/) and
[CANVAS](/blog/case-study-canvas/) — which between them have
consolidated a stack of licensed enterprise SaaS into a single,
audit-grade, internally-built system.

Before that, enterprise architecture governance at
[Aviva](https://www.aviva.co.uk/), global SAP S/4HANA architecture
at [Nestlé](https://www.nestle.com/), and a series of earlier
roles across multi-platform programmes in UK energy, financial
services and consumer goods.

## What I write about

Three streams, broadly.

**AI-native enterprise architecture.** The practical patterns for
deploying generative AI and agentic systems in regulated enterprise
contexts — governance, audit, evaluation, the actual policy you
need for AI coding tools, the protocols and standards (MCP in
particular) that are quietly reshaping how systems integrate.

**Architecture practice.** The mechanics of running an architecture
function in 2026: Architecture Decision Records, fitness functions
for measuring architectural health, identity-first security
architecture, the realities of large-scale ERP transformation,
event-driven architecture and when it actually adds value, and the
particular patterns that fit acquisition-heavy companies.

**Case studies.** Deeper technical pieces on systems I have built
end-to-end. Meridian and CANVAS are the major ones. Springboard
(an AI-native job-search platform) is the most recent.

## Things I have built

The most current and visible:

- **[Meridian](/blog/case-study-meridian/)** — internal AI-native enterprise
  architecture platform. Next.js 15, TypeScript, FastAPI, PostgreSQL on
  Azure, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Gemini for the conversational
  assistant. Replacing a stack of licensed enterprise SaaS (~$170K/year
  in savings at steady state). Includes the [CANVAS](/blog/case-study-canvas/)
  approval workflow folded in as a first-class surface.
- **[CANVAS](/blog/case-study-canvas/)** — the Central Application aNd
  Vendor Approval System. Nuxt 3, Flask, PostgreSQL, Microsoft Entra
  ID, Azure OpenAI. Ten-step workflow from application intake through
  contract signing, with append-only audit log, single-use SLT email
  approval tokens, and AI-generated architecture memos.
- **[Springboard](/blog/case-study-springboard/)** — solo-built AI-native
  job-search platform. ~15K lines, one engineer. Next.js 15 +
  Drizzle + Supabase + a fleet of GitHub Actions cron workers.
- Architecture governance functions at multiple organisations, the
  most recent built end-to-end from zero.
